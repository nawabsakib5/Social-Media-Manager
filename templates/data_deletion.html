Data Deletion Instructions

SocialManager Enterprise

If you want to delete your data from SocialManager, you have two options:

Option 1: Delete via Platform

1
Log in to your SocialManager account
2
Go to Accounts page
3
Disconnect all connected social media accounts
4
Contact us at info.cadobd@gmail.com to request full account deletion

Option 2: Facebook Data Deletion

If you connected SocialManager via Facebook Login and want to delete your Facebook data:

1
Go to Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites
2
Find SocialManager in the list
3
Click Remove → Check "Delete all posts, videos and events..."
4
Click Remove to confirm

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account and profile information
  • All connected social media account tokens
  • Inbox messages and conversation history
  • Published posts and scheduling data
  • All analytics and reporting data

Processing Time

Data deletion requests are processed within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once completed.

Contact

For data deletion requests: info.cadobd@gmail.com
