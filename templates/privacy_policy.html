Privacy Policy

SocialManager Enterprise

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Introduction

SocialManager ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our social media management platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and login credentials
  • Social Media Data: Posts, comments, messages, and engagement metrics from connected social media accounts
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our platform
  • Meta Platform Data: Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts, and Messenger conversations you authorize us to access

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our social media management services
  • To display and manage your social media content
  • To send and receive messages on your behalf through connected accounts
  • To generate analytics and insights about your social media performance
  • To communicate with you about our services

4. Meta Platform Data

When you connect your Facebook or Instagram account, we access:

  • Facebook Pages you manage
  • Instagram Business accounts linked to your Pages
  • Messages and comments on your Pages
  • Post engagement data

We only access data necessary to provide our services. We do not sell your Meta platform data to third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. Access tokens are encrypted using Fernet symmetric encryption.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except with your explicit consent, legal obligations, or trusted service providers.
